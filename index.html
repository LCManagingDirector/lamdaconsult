Who We Are

A Consortium Built on
Three Decades of Expertise

LAMDA Consult Ltd is a multidisciplinary engineering and architectural consulting firm registered in Ghana (Company No. 50,008), with its head office at Zoe Plaza, Dansoman-Accra, and a branch office in Monrovia, Liberia, and Koforidua.

Founded in 1993 as a consortium of senior engineers, architects and quantity surveyors, the firm has built a distinguished portfolio spanning roads and highways, bridges, water infrastructure, housing developments, institutional buildings, and large-scale project management across Ghana and the wider West Africa sub-region.

Our work is underpinned by rigorous engineering standards, deep local knowledge, and long-standing relationships with government agencies, multilateral development banks, and private sector clients. We bring international expertise to every engagement while remaining firmly rooted in the communities we serve.
